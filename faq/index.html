Getting there

The land

Over 200 acres of trails, a swimming pond and the place where two rivers meet. The full map shows camping, stages, the yoga shala and the fire circle.

Open the mapDirections
Where is the festival?

Sacred Mountain Waters Wellness Sanctuary, 2932 Big Laurel Rd, Marshall, NC 28753, about 40 minutes north of Asheville.

Will my phone work on the drive in?

Signal drops as you get close, so load GPS directions before you leave and follow the event signs once you arrive.

When can I arrive and when do I leave?

General admission opens Friday at 10 AM. Contributor Pass holders can arrive Thursday from 3 PM. Everyone departs by 6 PM on Sunday.

Leave extra time for parking, check-in and setting up camp.

Where do I park?

Without a car camping pass, cars go in the remote lot, which isn't far. Car camping and RV passes let you park at your site.

Camping & RV

Can I camp on site?

Yes. Tent camping is included with your pass. Creekside camping is part of the Contributor Pass.

Car camping and RV spots

A limited number of on-site car camping and RV spots are available. Reserve yours ahead of time.

Food

What food is available?

Food vendors run all weekend, including Connection Cafe's Buddha Bowls & Breakfast, Pasta & Prana hemp pasta, Silvermoon Cacao and Chocolate, and herbal elixirs by Laya Botanicals.

Artisans and healers also offer massage, acupuncture and private sound healing.

Is there a meal plan?

Contributor Passes include every meal of the weekend in the LovEvolution Lodge dining hall, prepared by a local chef. Brunch runs 11 AM to 1 PM in the Dining Den.

Is there childcare?

No. Children stay in their parents' care, and kids' sessions are run with parents nearby.

Tickets

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are non-refundable, but they are exchangeable. You can transfer yours to someone else.

Are there day passes?

A limited number of Friday and Saturday day passes are released closer to the festival.

What is the Contributor Pass?

The early supporter pass: a weekend pass with all meals, the artist lounge, creekside camping, all-access entry and Thursday early arrival.
